# AudioShelf privacy

AudioShelf stores saved-book metadata, listening position, playback preferences,
and downloaded chapters locally on the device. It does not include analytics,
advertising, user accounts, or telemetry.

Catalogue and audio requests are sent directly to the selected source. The bundled
source is LibriVox; cover fallback requests may be sent to Internet Archive. Those
services receive ordinary network information such as the device IP address and
the app's user-agent string.

Uninstalling the app removes its private settings and app-specific downloads.
